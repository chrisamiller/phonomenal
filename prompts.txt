# Writing prompts: one per line. Delete or edit any you don't want; lines starting with # are ignored.
# Each should be answerable in 3-4 sentences.

# Imagine / what if
If you could have any superpower for one day, what would you do with it?
Invent a new holiday. What is it called, and how do people celebrate it?
If you could talk to one kind of animal, which would you pick and what would you ask it?
Describe your dream bedroom. What would be in it?
If you could live inside any book, movie, or game, which would it be and why?
You find a door in your house that wasn't there yesterday. What's behind it?
If you could design a new school subject, what would students learn?
What would you do if you were invisible for an afternoon?
Invent a gadget that would make mornings easier. How does it work?
If you could visit any planet, which one would you choose and what would you pack?
You get to plan a perfect Saturday with no limits. What do you do?
If your pet (or a pet you'd like to have) could write a diary, what would today's entry say?
Imagine you are the principal for one day. What changes would you make?
If you could shrink to the size of an ant, where would you explore first?
You wake up and you are 30 years old. What does your day look like?
Describe a new flavor of ice cream you would invent.
If you could build a treehouse with anything you wanted, what would it have?
Invent a new sport. What are the rules?
If you could be any animal for a week, what would you be and why?
You are given a robot helper. What jobs would you give it?
If you could open any kind of shop, what would you sell?
You find a map that leads to treasure in your town. Where does it lead?
If you could swap lives with someone for a day, who would it be?
Describe a creature that lives at the bottom of the ocean that no one has discovered yet.
If you could make one rule that everyone in the world had to follow, what would it be?

# Opinions
What is the best season of the year, and why?
Should kids have homework on weekends? Explain your opinion.
Is it better to be a morning person or a night person? Why?
Which is better: cats or dogs? Give at least two reasons.
Should every school have a garden? Why or why not?
What is the most useful invention ever made? Explain.
Is it better to read the book or watch the movie first?
Should video games count as a sport? Why or why not?
What is the best pizza topping, and why are you right?
Would you rather live in the city or the countryside? Why?
Should students be allowed to choose their own seats in class?
What is the most underrated food?
Is it better to have one best friend or a lot of friends?
Should school start later in the morning? Explain.
What makes a good teacher?
What is the best way to spend a rainy day?
Should everyone learn to cook? Why or why not?
What is the hardest job in the world, and why?
Would you rather be really good at one thing or pretty good at many things?
Is it more fun to plan a trip or to actually go on it?

# Would you rather
Would you rather be able to fly or breathe underwater? Explain.
Would you rather visit the past or the future? Where would you go?
Would you rather have a pet dragon or a pet unicorn? Why?
Would you rather live in a castle or a spaceship?
Would you rather never be too hot or never be too cold?
Would you rather be able to speak every language or play every instrument?
Would you rather explore a jungle or a desert?
Would you rather have a rewind button or a pause button for your life?
Would you rather be the funniest person in the room or the smartest?
Would you rather spend a day at an amusement park or a water park?

# About you
What is something you are really good at? How did you get good at it?
Describe a time you were proud of yourself.
What is your favorite way to relax after a long day?
What is something new you would like to learn this year?
Describe your favorite place to be. What makes it special?
What is a goal you have for this year, and how will you reach it?
Tell about a time you tried something new. How did it go?
What is your favorite memory from last summer?
What are three things that always make you smile?
Who is someone you admire, and why?
What is the best gift you have ever given someone?
Describe a time you helped someone.
What is a skill you think every person should have?
What is your favorite book or series, and why should someone read it?
If you could have dinner with any character from a book or movie, who would it be?
What is something that is hard for you but that you keep working on?
Describe your perfect breakfast.
What is a tradition your family has that you enjoy?
What would you like to be when you grow up, and why?
What is a song that always puts you in a good mood? Why?

# Explain / describe
Explain how to make your favorite snack, step by step.
Describe your favorite animal to someone who has never seen it.
Explain the rules of your favorite game.
Describe what you see out the nearest window.
Explain how to be a good friend.
Describe the best day you can remember.
Explain why sleep is important.
Describe a storm using all five senses.
Explain how to take care of a pet.
Describe your town to someone who has never been there.
Explain something you know a lot about.
Describe a meal you would cook for your family.
Explain what you would pack for a week-long camping trip.
Describe the inside of a spaceship.
Explain how you would teach someone to ride a bike.
